Use a GLB from Tripo, Rodin or Meshy. It should be in metres and under about 5 MB.

What to check in AR

After tapping "View in your space", point the camera at a table and move the phone slowly from side to side until the surface is found. The ring then appears on it.

  1. Place it next to a real ring at true size. Does the size match?
  2. Walk around it. Does the gold reflect, or look like plastic?
  3. Get close. Do the beads and the stone hold up?
  4. Change metal and stone, then reopen AR. Do the swaps carry over?
